# Diagnostic Patterns

Use these as investigation paths, not canned fixes.

## Spend Stable, Value Or Revenue Down

Check conversion lag, tracking or consent issues, goal changes, product availability, price or offer changes, landing-page/checkout failures, query or placement drift, sibling campaign cannibalization, seasonality, and conversion-value import delays.

## Spend Up, Efficiency Down

Check budget or bid changes, learning status, search term drift, geo/device/daypart mix, audience expansion, final URL expansion, broad match, auto-apply changes, PMax asset or category expansion, and policy-limited campaigns losing high-intent traffic.

## Clicks Up, Conversions Down

Check query intent, landing-page relevance, form or booking flow issues, page speed, mobile usability, broken links, checkout errors, tracking failures, and mismatch between ad promise and page content.

## Conversion Goal Warnings

Open the campaign goal settings and account-level conversion diagnostics. Capture primary and secondary actions before proposing a change. Fix only after proving the campaign is optimizing to the wrong or missing business outcome.

## Search Term Cleanup

Export full search term evidence, group terms by intent, identify waste with spend and zero/low value, check for brand and sibling campaign conflicts, then propose narrowly scoped negatives or exclusions.

## Landing Page Suspected

Use the web launch review workflow for visual, mobile, speed, form, and conversion checks. Paid traffic performance problems often need both ad-platform evidence and page-level evidence.

## Ads And Analytics Disagree

Use cross-channel reconciliation. Compare attribution windows, conversion date basis, source/medium tagging, GA4 key events, ecommerce purchases, booking revenue, refunds, and import delays before assuming either platform is wrong.
