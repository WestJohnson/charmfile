# Change Safety

Before any live ad account change:

1. State the exact change in plain language.
2. Confirm the campaign, ad group, asset group, conversion goal, or setting being changed.
3. Confirm no budget increase unless explicitly approved.
4. Confirm no broad match expansion, AI Max, auto-apply, or automated asset generation unless explicitly approved.
5. Confirm the change is scoped to the affected business, campaign, product, service, geography, and intent.
6. Capture before-state.
7. Apply one logical change at a time.
8. Refresh and verify saved after-state.
9. Record rollback instructions.

If evidence is incomplete, prefer read-only export or inspection over guessing.

## Extra Gates

- Negative keywords or exclusions: export full query evidence, check sibling campaign/product conflicts, and scope lists narrowly.
- Conversion goal changes: inspect current primary and secondary actions before saving; never guess from a warning alone.
- Bid strategy or target changes: capture current strategy, target, budget, conversion volume, and learning status.
- Budget changes: require explicit budget approval and include old and new daily or lifetime amounts.
- PMax or automated campaign changes: check asset groups, final URL expansion, brand exclusions, search term/category evidence, and conversion goals first.
