# Evidence Checklist

Before recommending action, capture:

1. Account and business name.
2. Platform, account ID or visible account selector, and currency.
3. Date range and comparison range.
4. Attribution setting or conversion date basis when visible.
5. Spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, CPA, ROAS, conversion value, and revenue where available.
6. Campaign-level movers by spend, conversion value, ROAS, CPA, and conversion count.
7. Segment evidence when relevant: network, device, location, hour/day, audience, asset group, listing group, product/category, placement, or search term.
8. Search term or query evidence when relevant. Export the full report when the visible UI only shows a sample.
9. Conversion goal, primary/secondary action, tracking, consent, tag, or diagnostics warnings.
10. Budget, bid strategy, target, learning status, limited status, final URL expansion, auto-apply, broad match, and AI Max settings for affected campaigns.
11. Recent change history for affected campaigns when performance moved suddenly.
12. Landing page and final URL evidence when traffic quality or conversion rate changed.
13. Any account warnings, policy notices, disapprovals, payment warnings, or limited-status messages.

Do not infer live account state from old chat history when Chrome or exported reports are available.
Do not treat screenshots without account, date range, and column context as complete evidence.
