# SEO Checklist

Check at minimum:

1. Title and meta description exist and match the business/location/offer.
2. One clear H1 exists.
3. Local business name, service, and location are visible in body content when relevant.
4. Internal links and primary navigation work.
5. Images have useful alt text where appropriate.
6. Page has enough crawlable text to explain the business.
7. Structured data is present when appropriate.
8. Social/open graph metadata is present for production pages.
9. No placeholder copy, lorem ipsum, or fake contact data remains.

For local businesses, verify city/island/neighborhood language is natural and not keyword-stuffed.
