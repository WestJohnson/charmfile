# Analytics Diagnostics

Use these as investigation paths, not conclusions.

## Revenue Or Purchases Down

Check traffic mix, landing page conversion rate, checkout or booking funnel dropoff, tracking changes, consent effects, payment failures, item/service availability, price changes, refunds, attribution shifts, and late-arriving data.

## Traffic Stable, Revenue Down

Check source quality, landing-page intent, device mix, booking/checkout errors, product/service mix, offer changes, conversion tracking, and whether high-value channels dropped while low-value traffic rose.

## Paid Traffic Disagrees With GA4

Check auto-tagging, UTM overrides, cross-domain tracking, referral exclusions, consent mode, imported conversions, attribution model/window, conversion date basis, and GA4 key event definitions.

## Landing Page Problems

Segment by landing page, device, source/medium, campaign, and conversion event. If a page looks suspect, use the web launch review workflow to verify mobile UI, navigation, forms, page speed, and calls to action.

## Funnel Dropoff

Verify each step fires consistently. Watch for missing events after redirects, third-party booking engines, payment domains, iframe flows, cookie/consent boundaries, and confirmation pages that can be refreshed or skipped.

## Data Quality

Check duplicate events, missing values, currency mismatches, transaction ID reuse, internal traffic, bot/referral spam, self-referrals, and inconsistent UTM naming.
