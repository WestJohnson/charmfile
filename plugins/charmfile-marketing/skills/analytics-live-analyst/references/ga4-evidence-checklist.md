# GA4 Evidence Checklist

Before explaining analytics movement, capture:

1. Account, property, stream, report name, and date range.
2. Comparison range and whether data is finalized or still processing.
3. Filters, comparisons, segments, channel grouping, and attribution/reporting identity when visible.
4. Users, sessions, engaged sessions, engagement rate, conversions/key events, purchases/bookings, revenue, AOV, and conversion rate where relevant.
5. Source/medium, campaign, default channel group, landing page, device, location, and item/service breakdowns.
6. Funnel step counts for critical paths such as landing page, product/service page, booking/checkout, payment, and purchase/confirmation.
7. Event/key event definitions and whether the event is marked as a key event.
8. Ecommerce or booking parameters: transaction ID, item name/category, value, currency, quantity, and refunds when available.
9. Tag health or diagnostics warnings from GA4, GTM, Google tag, or platform integrations.
10. Recent changes to tracking, site releases, booking engines, redirects, consent banners, UTM conventions, or payment flows.

Do not compare Google Ads conversions to GA4 purchases without noting attribution windows, conversion date basis, modeled conversions, and import delays.
