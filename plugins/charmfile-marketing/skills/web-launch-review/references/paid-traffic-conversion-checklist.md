# Paid Traffic Conversion Checklist

Use this when a landing page affects ad performance.

1. Confirm the ad promise, landing page headline, offer, service, geography, and call to action match.
2. Verify mobile navigation, primary CTA visibility, tap targets, sticky elements, and no overlapping UI.
3. Test the complete conversion path: click CTA, form, booking widget, cart, checkout, phone link, map link, and confirmation state as applicable.
4. Check that final URLs, UTMs, redirects, canonical URLs, and tracking parameters survive page loads and CTA clicks.
5. Inspect load speed, layout shift, image weight, third-party scripts, and booking widget delays on mobile.
6. Confirm trust signals: reviews, location, hours, policies, pricing or starting price, availability, and contact details.
7. Check that the page handles campaign intent: brand, category, product/service, competitor, local, emergency, or informational.
8. Verify forms and widgets produce visible success states and do not silently fail.
9. Note tracking requirements for GA4, Google tag, GTM, ad pixels, call tracking, and booking confirmation events, but do not change them without approval.

Output page issues as conversion blockers, measurement blockers, trust blockers, or polish issues.
